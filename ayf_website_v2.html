Body Fat Estimate — 3 Point Pinch Test
📏 How to do the pinch test:

Pinch your skin firmly (not muscle) with thumb and index finger. Measure the fold thickness in mm.

Thigh — Front of thigh, halfway between knee and hip
Stomach — 2cm to the right of your belly button
Back of Arm — Midpoint between shoulder and elbow

Rough guide: thin fold ≈ 5–10, medium ≈ 15–25, thick ≈ 30+
